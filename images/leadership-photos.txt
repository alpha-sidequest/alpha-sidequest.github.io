RAAF Knowledge Hub - Leadership Photos for Ranks Section
=======================================================

The "Current Senior Leadership" section now includes six important officials. You will need official photos for all of them.

Required images (place inside the images/ folder):

1. Minister for Defence
   Filename: richard-marles.jpg
   Recommended source: Official Australian Government / Department of Defence portrait

2. Chief of the Defence Force
   Filename: david-johnston.jpg
   Recommended source: Official Defence portrait of Admiral David Johnston AC RAN

3. Chief of Air Force
   Filename: stephen-chappell.jpg
   Recommended source: Official RAAF portrait of Air Marshal Stephen Chappell

4. Deputy Chief of Air Force
   Filename: steve-pesce.jpg
   Recommended source: Official RAAF portrait of Air Vice-Marshal Steve Pesce AM

5. Air Commander Australia
   Filename: harvey-reynolds.jpg
   Recommended source: Official RAAF portrait of Air Vice-Marshal Harvey Reynolds AM

6. Warrant Officer of the Air Force
   Filename: ralph-clifton.jpg
   Recommended source: Official RAAF portrait of WOFF-AF Ralph Clifton

Tips:
- Use high-resolution, official, professional head-and-shoulders or upper-body photos.
- Crop to a nice portrait ratio (roughly 4:3 or 3:2 works well).
- Keep file size reasonable (under 800KB each is fine).
- Official Defence / RAAF imagery is strongly preferred for accuracy.

Once the photos are added to the images/ folder with the exact filenames above, all six leadership cards will display correctly.

These six people represent the key civilian and military leaders you should be able to discuss in a defence interview.
