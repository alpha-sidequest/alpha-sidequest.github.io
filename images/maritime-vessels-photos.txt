RAAF Knowledge Hub - Maritime Vessels Photos
==============================================

This section covers major Royal Australian Navy vessels that RAAF maritime crews (especially P-8A Poseidon, MH-60R, and MQ-4C Triton) need to know.

Recommended images to add (place in images/ folder):

1. HMAS Hobart (Hobart-class Destroyer)
   Filename: hobart.jpg
   Best source: Official Defence imagery of HMAS Hobart at sea

2. HMAS Anzac (Anzac-class Frigate)
   Filename: anzac.jpg
   Best source: Official RAN photo of an Anzac-class frigate

3. HMAS Collins (Collins-class Submarine)
   Filename: collins.jpg
   Best source: Official photo of a Collins-class submarine on the surface

4. HMAS Canberra (Canberra-class LHD)
   Filename: canberra.jpg
   Best source: Official photo of HMAS Canberra or Adelaide with helicopters on deck

Additional useful images (optional but recommended):
- Supply-class replenishment ship (HMAS Supply or Stalwart)
- HMAS Choules (Bay-class LSD)
- Good side/profile views of Hobart-class and Anzac-class for recognition training

Tips:
- Use official Defence / Navy imagery where possible.
- Good clear photos from the side or three-quarter angles work best for recognition.
- Keep file sizes reasonable (under 800KB).
- Prioritise recent, high-quality images.

Once added, the maritime cards will look much more professional and consistent with the Aircraft section.
